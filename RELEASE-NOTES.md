## 4.2.0 — 2026-09-18

### Worth

- **Characters on connected realms are priced from the auction house those realms share.**
  Connected realms trade in one auction house, goblin houses included. A price read on any
  realm of the group now counts for your characters on all of them, and the most recent
  reading wins. Before, an alt on a second realm of the group had no auction prices and was
  valued at vendor prices. An item you banned on one realm is banned for the whole group.
  Family learns the group from the game, so log in once on any realm of it.

### Professions

- **Hovering a material on a recipe row now describes that material.** Each recipe row shows
  its materials as icons. Hover one and you get that item's own tooltip, with who in the
  family already has some. Before, the icon showed the tooltip of the thing being made.
  Clicking an icon still does what clicking the row does.

### Searching for an item

- **CTRL-ALT-click on a chat link now searches in your own language.** A link carries the
  item's name in the sender's language. An English player's link made a French client search
  for the English name, and the page came back empty. Family now asks the game what the item
  is called in your language. It uses the link's own words only when your game cannot name
  the item.

### On the game's tooltips

- **Worth now says in words how many copies were priced each way.** The lines under Worth
  read *574 at vendor prices* and *12 at auction prices*. Before, the count sat in the money
  column and looked like a price. The summary's Worth tooltip changed the same way. No figure
  changed.

- **The CTRL-ALT-click shortcut is mentioned on every item somebody owns.** CTRL-ALT-click on
  an item opens Family on everyone who has one. The tooltip used to mention it only when the
  list of owners was too long to show in full. It now appears in grey under the owners, on
  any item the family holds. It is left off action bar buttons, where those keys belong to
  the bar.
