## 5.2.0 — 2026-10-31

### Professions

- **The whole-family recipe search shows every transmute**, including the two that make the same
  essence as another, such as *Life to Earth* beside *Fire to Earth*, each under its own name in
  your language even when it was recorded in another. A smelting recipe and a transmute that
  make the same bar are now two rows as well. Before, the search kept only one of
  each pair; an alchemist's own page always showed them all. Open each alchemist's profession
  window once for their transmutes to be listed apart.
